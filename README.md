# Paytm FinTech Analytics & AI Platform

A unified FinTech analytics capstone covering Paytm-style payments operations,
credit-risk machine learning, and AI-augmented financial advisory.

## Project Structure

### 1. Payments & Fraud Analytics

Located in:

`/payments_fraud_analytics`

Covers merchant analytics, SQL fraud detection, payment reconciliation,
and dashboarding.

### 2. Credit Risk & Lending ML

Located in:

`/credit_risk_lending_ml`

Covers credit-risk modelling, risk-based pricing, anomaly detection,
and model governance.

### 3. AI Advisory & Blockchain Risk

Located in:

`/ai_advisory_blockchain`

Covers portfolio advisory, structured disclosure extraction,
multi-agent analysis, DCF valuation, and blockchain/crypto risk.
